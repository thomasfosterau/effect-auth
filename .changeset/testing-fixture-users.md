`@thomasfosterau/effect-auth/testing` adds fixture users with UUID ids: `fixtureUsers()` (an admin, a member and a second member, with fixed v4 UUIDs in `fixtureUserIds`) and `fixtureUser()` (one user, a fresh random UUID by default). Ids, roles (`attributes.role`) and timestamps are overridable.
